# Why Zyvor

## The buyer problem

Enterprises are forced to rethink virtualization and private cloud:

- License shocks and vendor lock-in on proprietary hypervisors / HCI
- Public-cloud bills that outran the original migration thesis
- Fragmented ops: export tools, converters, KubeVirt consoles, GPU stacks, and “hope the webhook ran”

Zyvor sells a **suite**, not a single converter: migration → run → operate → scale, with Community Edition tools where open source belongs.

## What we are not

- Not a contractual “99.99% forever” slogan product
- Not a VMware clone for its own sake
- Not a pure SaaS that requires our cloud to run your control plane (edge / on-prem first for products like Relay)

## What we are

- **Kubernetes-native and KVM-honest** — KubeVirt, libvirt, and open formats as first-class citizens
- **Operator-shaped UX** — manuals, consoles, and trials on [zyvor.dev](https://zyvor.dev)
- **Commercial where it matters** — Enterprise EULA / support on control planes; Apache-2.0 on Community tools
